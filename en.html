Privacy Policy

Siemvist for Android · Developer: Azmyd (Colombia) · Effective November 15, 2026

In one sentence: Siemvist keeps everything on your device. The app does not send any of its own data to any server, has no user accounts, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or tracking tools.

1. What Siemvist does

Siemvist helps you remember where you keep your things. You photograph an object, say or type where it is, and when you need it, you search by name or by voice. All processing happens on your phone.

2. Data stored on your device

This information exists only in the app's private storage on your phone. We have no access to it.

DataWhat it is used for
Photos of your objectsIdentifying the object in the list and in search.
Name, place described by you, notes and tagsFinding the object later. The "place" is text you type or dictate; the app does not use GPS and does not request location permission.
RemindersShowing you local notifications on the date you choose.
Private zonePrivate objects are protected with a PIN. Only a cryptographic digest (hash) of the PIN is stored in Android's secure storage, never the PIN itself. If you use fingerprint or face unlock, Android performs the verification; the app does not access your biometric data.
Settings and Premium purchase statusRemembering your preferences and whether you own Premium.

3. Data the app sends

None. Siemvist does not send your photos, text, recordings or any other data of its own to Azmyd's servers or anyone else's. The app has no server of its own.

The app uses the internet only in the following cases, and none of them carries your information:

  • AI model download (first time). To recognize objects in photos, the app downloads two artificial-intelligence models once (about 90 MB) from Hugging Face, published by Software Mansion. After that, recognition runs on your phone, offline.
  • Translation model download. To translate object labels into your language, the app uses Google's on-device ML Kit. The first time, it downloads the language model from Google; the translated text never leaves your phone.
  • Premium purchase. Google Play processes the payment (see section 5).
  • Voice dictation on Android 12 or lower. See section 4.

4. Voice dictation

When you tap the microphone, dictation is processed by the Android system's speech recognition service (usually Google's), not by Siemvist. The app receives only the resulting text and neither stores nor sends the audio.

On Android 12 or lower, that system service may use the internet to transcribe your voice, depending on your device settings and the provider's privacy policy. On Android 13 or higher, the system usually processes speech on the device itself. If you prefer not to use dictation, you can type with the keyboard: no feature depends on voice.

5. Premium purchase (Google Play)

Premium is a one-time payment that raises the number of objects you can save. The payment is handled by Google Play under its own privacy policy. Siemvist only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase exists and stores it on your device. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

6. Backups

You can export a copy of your data. The file is encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256) and handed to the app you select to save or share it. That file never passes through us. Without the password, no one can open it, not even Azmyd.

8. Third parties

Siemvist integrates no advertising, analytics or social-network SDKs. The only external services involved are: Google Play (purchases and translation model download), Hugging Face (AI model download) and the Android system's speech recognition service. Each is governed by its own privacy policy.

9. Children

Siemvist is intended for a general audience and is not designed for children under 13. Since we do not collect data, we do not process information about children.

10. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. You can delete any object from the app. Uninstalling Siemvist removes all of the app's data. There is no copy on any server for us to delete.

11. Security

Data lives in the app's private space, which Android isolates from other applications. The private-zone PIN is stored as a hash in the system's secure storage. Backups are encrypted with your password.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data, we will update this page and state the new effective date.
